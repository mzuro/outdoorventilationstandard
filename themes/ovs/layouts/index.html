2.1

What CFM do I need?

Not a rule of thumb. Airflow only matters once the hood face can geometrically intercept the plume — width and height come first, CFM is derived after.

RB-008 · Instrument I-01 2.2

Does wind matter?

More than any other variable. A steady 5 mph breeze can displace the plume by half a hood-width before it reaches the capture plane.

RB-006 · Instrument I-03 2.3

Island or wall hood?

A wall behind the grill is a free side panel: it blocks one entrainment path and one wind direction. Islands need wider hoods, mounted lower, to match.

RB-005, RB-009 · Instrument I-04
